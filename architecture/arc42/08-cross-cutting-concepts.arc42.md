# 8. Cross-cutting Concepts

<!-- arc42:section 08 -->

Cross-cutting concepts include:

- stable IDs and versioned schemas;
- repository-ID identity and origin-scoped credentials;
- semantic proposal followed by deterministic enforcement;
- GitHub Issues as source of truth and Projects as projection;
- provenance, content hashes, and immutable release references;
- explicit secret and tool/MCP allow-lists;
- replay protection and per-origin execution-state namespaces; and
- human authorization for merge and architecture activation.

The durable principles are referenced from `../principles/index.yml`.
