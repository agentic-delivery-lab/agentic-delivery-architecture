# 12. Glossary

The machine-readable ubiquitous-language register is
`../domain/ubiquitous-language.yml`. This chapter explains how to read it and
does not maintain a second list of terms.

Terms with different meanings in Architecture Authority, Delivery Control
Plane, or Agentic Primitives must be qualified by context rather than silently
merged.
