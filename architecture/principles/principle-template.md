# AP-NNN — Short principle name

## Statement

State one concise architectural principle with an observable consequence.

## Goals and decision basis

Identify the architecture goal IDs. Link the decisions and consequences that
support the principle, and explain the evidence connecting them.

## Consequences

- State a supported positive, negative, or neutral effect.
- Keep context-specific implementation ownership explicit.

## Evidence and current limits

Link immutable source revisions and paths. Separate current behavior from
target contracts and state evidence limits.
