# Strategy foundation research register

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-11. This register records source-backed findings used by
the proposed strategy and architecture. Official product documentation
establishes documented features and constraints; the live GitHub observations
remain separately recorded in `system-evidence.yml`. Public project documents
are examples of author practice, not standards or proof of outcomes.

| Strategy implementation — [Sull, Homkes, and Sull (2021), review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.emj.2020.09.005), European Management Journal | The systematic review treats implementation as a multi-part, dynamic process involving managerial action and enabling conditions; it notes that strategy implementation is often treated as a black box. | A cross-sector review does not validate a particular operating model for this lab. | Make the translation from strategy to goals, prioritized Issues, delivery changes, measures, and review evidence explicit so execution stays inspectable. |

| Measure selection — [Kershaw and Malhotra (2012), empirical study](https://doi.org/10.1504/IJAAPE.2012.043964), publisher abstract | Their study reports that strategy-aligned measures may focus managerial effort, while a highly responsive secondary measure can divert effort from primary goals. | The result is context-specific and does not directly measure agentic delivery behavior. | Treat metric gaming and local optimization as risks; report baselines, denominators, uncertainty, and complementary user, quality, security, and delivery measures. |
